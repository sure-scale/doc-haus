- **Drafting follows the plan.** A combined Word file now builds from the plan's authored memo, so a cover memo and the operative documents stay in the intended order. Companion instruments follow the delivery instruction: they stay with the named file, or arrive separately when you ask for that.
- **Quotes resolve to the exact source.** Drafting evidence now resolves to precise source spans in code, so a sound quotation is not rejected because of a span mismatch.
- **Long drafting notes finish.** A section that needs several source lookups can use the run's full lookup budget instead of stopping at the first ceiling. Brief database locks are waited out, and research findings that arrive without IDs are numbered instead of failing the run.
- **Disclosed gaps in review drafts.** With drafting Auto mode enabled as an installation opt-in, intake continues through non-blocking missing facts as visible placeholders and open items in the drafting notes. Explicit holds, indispensable facts and choices reserved to the lawyer still stop the run and ask.
- **Cleaner review and repair.** The provisional-marker duty is checked earlier, rejected note sections are corrected in place, and one document catalog keeps notes coverage consistent.
- **Full review and dashboards stay in one flow.** Their helper agents run each step to completion and pass the findings straight to the next, so a review report or dashboard plan arrives in a single response.
- **A more reliable engine.** The underlying engine is updated to OpenCode v2.4.2. Provider streams retry cleanly after a dropped connection, interrupted sessions resume after a restart, newer models and provider lists are handled correctly, and dependencies are updated.

Known limitations: the key-date register remains read-only in this release; calendar export, reminders and reviewed accept/cancel are later work. Removing inherited bold, italic or underline remains unsupported outside Word. Reopening a local Word file may require “Take over editing here” and a confirmation Save.
