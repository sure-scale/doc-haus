- **Review key dates in one source-linked register.** Renewals, notice windows, expiries, payment milestones and deliverables recorded during reviews now sit in one matter register. A resolved calendar date stays distinct from a relative formula that still needs an anchor, and a recorded date is not treated as a confirmed deadline until you check it.
- **See what backs an answer.** Q&A answers now show Grounded, Limited support or No verified support, and explain what was checked against the document. The note does not claim a passage answers the question.
- **Use more than one AI provider.** Add or remove providers from one list, and choose your primary and fast models from any connected provider.
- **Review-table refinements.** Undo a deleted comment, save your comment name once, see clearer Verified and source-changed signals, avoid a duplicate-upload failure, and read a comparison summary built from its group points.
- **Easier template sets.** When spreadsheet headings differ from a template's fields, doc.haus suggests a matching column with a Suggested mark. The Match step now shows each field and column as one tonal row.
- **Word clause history stays with the conversation.** Clause history now sits above the conversation in Word, so the two remain together while you review a change.
- **Reliability and security.** Background help keeps working while you do, interrupted work resumes once where it stopped, temporary connection problems are retried, previews and downloads are safer, research connections stay signed in, and newer models and provider lists behave better.

Known limitations: the key-date register is read-only in this release; calendar export, reminders and reviewed accept/cancel are later work. Removing inherited bold, italic or underline remains unsupported outside Word. Reopening a local Word file may require “Take over editing here” and a confirmation Save.
