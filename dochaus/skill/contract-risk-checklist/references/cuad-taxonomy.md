# CUAD 41-category taxonomy mapped to the 12 checklist areas

The Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD) labels 41 clause categories
across 510 real commercial contracts. Use these category names as the
canonical clause vocabulary when classifying what a clause is, then review it
under the mapped checklist area. CUAD is built from US (SEC-filed) contracts;
the US-only caveat applies.

Source: The Atticus Project,
[CUAD](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The category names
below are reproduced from CUAD; doc.haus adds the mapping to its 12 checklist
areas and the review guidance and coverage notes. The source material retains

## Coverage notes

- CUAD has no categories for indemnification scope, confidentiality
  obligations themselves, force majeure, or data protection — the checklist
  covers those areas independently of CUAD.
- CUAD's restrictive-covenant categories (Non-Compete, Exclusivity,
  No-Solicit, Non-Disparagement) have no dedicated checklist area; they are
  grouped under area 6 because in commercial review they most often travel
  with the confidentiality cluster. Their enforceability is highly
  jurisdiction-dependent — always qualify.
